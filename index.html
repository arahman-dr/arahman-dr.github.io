About

I am an MSCA Fellow in the PhotoBrane Doctoral Network, where I research photo-responsive membrane systems for molecular separation. PhotoBrane brings together membrane technology, materials science and photochemistry to build membranes whose properties are controlled by light. The aims are better separation performance, less fouling and longer membrane lifetime.

I study these systems with spectroscopy, from UV–Vis to transient absorption (TA), to understand the photophysical behaviour and light-induced processes of materials used in photo-tunable membranes. I am especially interested in how material structure, photophysical processes and membrane function connect.

My background is in materials engineering, followed by the Erasmus Mundus Master's in Membrane Engineering for Sustainable Development. Working in an international, interdisciplinary network lets me collaborate across fields and broaden my scientific perspective.

Interests: photo-responsive membranes · molecular separation · membrane fouling · transient absorption spectroscopy · ion-exchange membranes · water treatment

Research projects

  • Photo-responsive membranes (PhotoBrane). Light-controlled membranes for molecular separation, studied with UV–Vis and transient absorption spectroscopy. Leibniz IOM, ongoing.
  • Monovalent ion selectivity in PSS/PVAm saloplastics. Improving K⁺/Na⁺ selectivity and mechanical stability of hot-pressed cation-exchange membranes through glutaraldehyde crosslinking. Master's thesis, University of Twente.
  • Healing defects in ZIF membranes. Preparing Zn₂(bim)₄ zeolitic imidazolate framework membranes for H₂/CO₂ separation and repairing defects with interfacial polymerization. Inorganic Membranes group, University of Twente.

  • Green-synthesized magnetite nanoparticles. Co-precipitation using leaf extract of Alternanthera philoxeroides as a reducing agent, for wastewater treatment. Bachelor's thesis, University of Rajshahi.
  • Eggshell waste to soda-lime glass. Synthesizing and optimizing CaO/CaCO₃ from eggshells and using it to make glass, with energy storage as a long-term aim. University of Rajshahi.
  • Lead borosilicate quenched glasses. Characterizing how lead content changes the properties of borosilicate glass. University of Rajshahi.
  • Perovskite solar cell performance. Numerical investigation of device performance in SCAPS-1D. University of Rajshahi.
