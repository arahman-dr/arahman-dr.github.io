Research

Membranes whose properties are controlled by light, and the materials science behind them.

Current: PhotoBrane

I am an MSCA Fellow in the PhotoBrane Doctoral Network, working in the research department “Surfaces of Porous Membrane Filters” at the Leibniz Institute of Surface Engineering (IOM) in Leipzig. My research is on photo-responsive membrane systems for molecular separation.

PhotoBrane brings together membrane technology, materials science and photochemistry to develop membranes whose properties can be controlled by light. The broader goals are better molecular separation performance, less fouling and longer membrane lifetime. I am especially interested in how material structure, photophysical processes and membrane function connect.

I explore these systems with spectroscopy, from UV–Vis to transient absorption (TA), which lets me investigate the photophysical behaviour and light-induced processes of materials used in photo-tunable membranes.

The network is funded by the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions under Horizon Europe. It gathers experts in membrane technology, dye chemistry, photoswitches and computational chemistry from across Europe and beyond. Doctoral researchers receive hands-on training in molecular modeling and in synthesizing photoswitchable building blocks for molecular layers and phototunable membranes. PhotoBrane website

Keywords: photo-responsive membranes · molecular separation · membrane fouling · photoswitches · UV–Vis and transient absorption spectroscopy

Earlier projects

  • Monovalent ion selectivity in PSS/PVAm saloplastics. Improving K⁺/Na⁺ selectivity and mechanical stability of hot-pressed cation-exchange membranes through glutaraldehyde crosslinking. Master's thesis, Twente Membranes, University of Twente.
  • Healing defects in ZIF membranes. Preparing Zn₂(bim)₄ zeolitic imidazolate framework membranes for H₂/CO₂ separation and repairing defects with interfacial polymerization. Inorganic Membranes group, University of Twente.
  • Mixed matrix membranes for heavy-metal removal. Incorporating MOFs into porous membranes to remove Pb²⁺ from simulated perovskite-contaminated rainwater, with the goal of reaching the WHO limit of 10 ppb. Universidad de Zaragoza.
  • Prussian blue cathodes for sodium-ion batteries. Synthesis and characterization of Prussian blue electrodes. INMA, Zaragoza.
  • Green-synthesized magnetite nanoparticles. Co-precipitation using leaf extract of Alternanthera philoxeroides as a reducing agent, for wastewater treatment. Bachelor's thesis, University of Rajshahi.
  • Eggshell waste to soda-lime glass. Synthesizing and optimizing CaO/CaCO₃ from eggshells and using it to make glass, with energy storage as a long-term aim. University of Rajshahi.
  • Lead borosilicate quenched glasses. Characterizing how lead content changes the properties of borosilicate glass. University of Rajshahi.
  • Perovskite solar cell performance. Numerical investigation of device performance in SCAPS-1D. University of Rajshahi.
